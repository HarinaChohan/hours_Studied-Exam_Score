## Predicting Student Exam Scores Using Study Hours

### Problem

Students always wonder if there is a point in spending time studying for an exam. Are the number of hours studied significant enough to get a good score?

Hence, the objective will be to build a model to predict the score of a student in their exam based on how many hours they spent studying and also to see its correlation (whether positive or negative) and make analysis and conclusions through them.

Hypothesis: The Hours spent studying is correlated to the Exam score a student gets.

Null hypothesis is that the exam score received has no correlation to the hours a student spent studying.
